- graft-mirror: batch mode (decision D21). `graft-mirror pr-batch <line> --base main` follows `main`, syncs an integration line onto it (rebase of the line's net change, recorded as a fast-forward merge changeset by CAS; conflicts reported, nothing forced), and exports the net change as one squashed commit on GitHub's `main`, pushed as `graft/batch-<n>` with a lease and opened as a PR listing every land (changeset, author, session, work item, attestations) with `Graft-Batch` trailers. The PR is never merged or queued by graft. A merged PR settles the batch once follow imports its merge commit, and the next batch counts from its tip; a PR closed unmerged marks the batch rejected and leaves the line as is.
