# G3.4: three-way text merge

- `graft-model::merge`: `merge3` returns `Clean`, `Conflicts` (line ranges in ours) or `NotText` (not UTF-8, a NUL byte, or 1 MiB or more; D16). It follows `git merge-file --diff3 --diff-algorithm=histogram`: git's histogram diff with xdiff's hunk sliding and `EAGER` combination. No new dependencies.
- `render_conflict_markers` writes git's diff3 markers (`ours` / `base` / `theirs`).
- Tests: a 600-case differential corpus against `git merge-file` (cleanliness and bytes; known differences listed in the test), and proptest invariants (unchanged side, identical changes, ours/theirs symmetry).
