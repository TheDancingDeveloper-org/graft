- `graft-mirror pr-batch` gains `--min-lands N`, `--min-age MINUTES` and `--force`, and a `[batch]` table in `mirror.toml` (`min_lands`, `min_age_minutes`, defaults 6 and 60). With thresholds set, a run cuts a batch only when enough lands wait or the previous batch settled long enough ago; otherwise it prints `batch deferred: …` and exits 0. `--force` cuts anyway and marks the PR body `forced`. Unset keeps today's behaviour.
