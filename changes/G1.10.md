# G1.10: history queries — Log, ancestry, Blame

- `graft-index`: `Index::log` answers first-parent history by walking a draft's first parents to the line, then by `line_paths` range scans (with paths or directories) or a skip-list jump and a first-parent walk (without); it pages by `after_pos` or by `from`, and reads O(results) index rows. `Index::is_ancestor` and `Index::merge_base` prune by generation and line position. `Index::blame` is a lazy kind cached per `(changeset, path)` (encoding pinned by a golden vector): it walks the file's versions back to the nearest cached value and carries line origins forward with a histogram diff (`imara-diff`).
- Tests: proptests against brute force (ancestry and merge base on random DAGs, Log on random DAGs with drafts and paging), read-count tests, and differential tests against `git log --first-parent` and `git blame --first-parent` on synthetic repositories and (ignored, network) rustTorrent.
