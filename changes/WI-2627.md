bench/postland records the v0.2 exit measurements. record.py follows a line from a stored LSN through `graft api` watch and get_land_checks, writes JSONL with observer timestamps, resumes without duplicate rows and flags late entries. plant.py lands a fmt or test plant and a good follower under the postland-plant identity and prints both changeset ids. report.py writes the M1 table (p95 ≤ 30 min, max ≤ 4 h, over-bound, late, pending, and Blocked time on its own), the M2 plant table, the M3 day table and the D31 before/after table from Prometheus `graft_rpc_duration_seconds{op="Land"}`. Tests use recorded fixtures with no sleeps.
