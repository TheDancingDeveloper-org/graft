- graft-mirror: a batch commit's `Claimed-Author` trailer no longer leaks a
  client-supplied email. It is dropped when the email matches
  `[export.authors]` or `default_author`; otherwise the email is kept only
  when its domain is on the new `[export] claimed_author_domains` allow-list
  (default `["users.noreply.github.com"]`), and the trailer keeps the name
  alone. Applies to batches cut from now on; merged commits are unchanged.
