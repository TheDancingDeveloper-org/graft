- `graft-mirror pr-batch` can drop a land from a batch without holding back the others (WI-2340). `--exclude <changeset>` (repeatable, with `--reason`) or `--drop-failed` (lands with a failed attestation) lands a graft `Revert` of it on the integration line, so the lands after it stay. An open batch is closed with a comment and recut from the same start; the new PR body lists each dropped land, its revert and the reason. A cut never splits a land from its revert.
- Batch cut policy: `--max-lands <n>` caps a batch (oldest lands first; the rest wait for the next batch, exported against the base the line held at the cut), and `--max-age <seconds>` holds a batch until its oldest land is that old or it is full. The mirror DB gains `batch_drops` and `batch_lines.start_base` (added in place on open).
